GILDED — The Midnight Vault Job: a top-down heist game in plain static files. Sneak Rook the thief through three vault rooms past patrolling guards and their vision cones, sweeping security cameras, and toggling laser banks; toss coins to lure guards away, jam cameras, kill lasers at wall switches, pocket every gold bar and the Star Diamond, and reach the getaway door before the alarm meter hits 100%.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sneak, click to toss a coin, J to jam the cameras. The screenshots in screenshots/ are staged scenes of the real game (`?shot=title|play|alarm|win`).
